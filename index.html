ABOUT THE POET

Words That Come From Within.

01

I am GEN-ESIS THE POET, a Malawian poet and creative voice writing from the realities of life.

I started writing poetry in 2016. Through words, I explore healing, inspiration, social consciousness, identity, love, faith, family and the experiences that shape who we become.

My poetry is a reflection of life — its pain, beauty, questions, struggles and hope.
